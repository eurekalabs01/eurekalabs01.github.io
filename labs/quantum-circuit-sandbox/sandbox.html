Each qubit on the Bloch sphere

Each sphere shows one qubit looked at on its own. An arrow on the surface means the qubit has a definite state of its own, like |0⟩ or |+⟩. When the qubit is entangled with others, the arrow shrinks toward the center. At the very center the qubit on its own is a 50/50 mix of 0 and 1: measuring it gives each result half the time, as with |+⟩, but the outcome is tied to the other qubits, and no gate on this qubit alone can make it definite again.
